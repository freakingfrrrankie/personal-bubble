Presence does not mean simply being physically located somewhere. It names the condition of becoming perceptible and consequential within a relation — not just being there, but registering as something a relation can act on.

Relationship names the structural condition itself: that one thing's state changes what is possible for another. Presence names something narrower — whether, and how, a particular body has actually become one of the terms within that structure. A body can be physically present in a space without being present to a given technological system, if nothing about it has registered within that system's sensing apparatus.
