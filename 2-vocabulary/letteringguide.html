After encountering an archive of lettering tools, I began thinking about how a lettering guide could carry more than a system for constructing letters. Together with Kristí Fekete, I developed this idea into a workshop at Aalto University, where participants created lettering guides embedded with personal narratives, using the shapes and traces of an object of their choice as a starting point.

In preparation, I made couple of guides of my own.
One grew from a wild plant in my garden, following the way its leaves developed and wove into one another.
Another came from the board game Catan, using the small road piece—the element that connects villages and cities—as its basic form. The typeface drawn from this second guide was later developed further and used in the handheld visit-card maker, becoming one of the first practical experiments of the research.
